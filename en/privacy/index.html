Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Oranoss Co., Ltd. ("the Company") respects your privacy and protects your personal data in accordance with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA).

Data we collect

When you submit a quotation request or contact form, we collect the details you provide: name, company, phone number, email and your enquiry.

Purpose

We use this data only to reply to you, prepare quotations and provide the services you request. We do not sell or disclose your data to third parties for marketing.

Retention

We keep data only as long as necessary to provide the service and as required by law.

Your rights
