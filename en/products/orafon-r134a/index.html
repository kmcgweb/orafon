ORAFON R-134a is an HFC refrigerant (single component), ASHRAE number R-134a, composition CH₂FCF₃ (1,1,1,2-Tetrafluoroethane). It is classified A1 under ASHRAE 34, with GWP (AR4) 1430 and ODP 0.

A single-component, non-flammable (A1) HFC used in legacy automotive AC, domestic refrigerators, medium-temperature refrigeration and chillers.

An ORAFON-brand product from Oranoss Co., Ltd., backed by TDS/SDS documents and technical advice on choosing the right refrigerant. Contact us for pack sizes, stock and a quotation.
