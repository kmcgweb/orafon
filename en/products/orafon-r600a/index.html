ORAFON R-600a is an HC refrigerant (single component), ASHRAE number R-600a, composition C₄H₁₀ (Isobutane). It is classified A3 under ASHRAE 34, with GWP (AR4) 3 and ODP 0.

A natural hydrocarbon refrigerant with very low GWP and good efficiency, used in modern domestic refrigerators and small freezers. Highly flammable (A3): use only in equipment designed for it, handled by trained technicians.

Caution: R-600a is classified A3 (highly flammable). Use it only in equipment designed for this class, never as a replacement in systems designed for A1 refrigerants, and have trained technicians handle it.

An ORAFON-brand product from Oranoss Co., Ltd., backed by TDS/SDS documents and technical advice on choosing the right refrigerant. Contact us for pack sizes, stock and a quotation.

Which refrigerants can ORAFON R-600a replace?

Is it flammable? How safe is it?
It is classified A3 under ASHRAE 34. Highly flammable: only for equipment designed for A3, handled by trained technicians.
