Solstice® R-452A is an HFO Blend refrigerant (zeotropic blend), ASHRAE number R-452A, composition R-32 / R-125 / R-1234yf (11/59/30). It is classified A1 under ASHRAE 34, with GWP (AR4) 2140 and ODP 0.

A non-flammable (A1) HFO/HFC blend designed for transport refrigeration such as reefer trucks and containers, replacing R-404A with similar compressor discharge temperatures and about 45% lower GWP.

Distributed in Thailand by ORAFON (Oranoss Co., Ltd.), an authorized Solstice® distributor, with TDS/SDS documents and technical support. Contact us for pack sizes, stock and a quotation.
