ORAFON R-245fa is an HFC refrigerant (single component), ASHRAE number R-245fa, composition CF₃CH₂CHF₂ (1,1,1,3,3-Pentafluoropropane). It is classified B1 under ASHRAE 34, with GWP (AR4) 1030 and ODP 0.

A low-pressure, single-component HFC that is non-flammable but in the higher-toxicity group (B1). Used in low-pressure chillers, Organic Rankine Cycle (ORC) systems and as a foam blowing agent; handle strictly according to safety requirements.

Caution: B1 refrigerants have higher toxicity. Install ventilation and leak detection, and use protective equipment as specified in the SDS.
