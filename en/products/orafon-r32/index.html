ORAFON R-32 is an HFC refrigerant (single component), ASHRAE number R-32, composition CH₂F₂ (Difluoromethane). It is classified A2L under ASHRAE 34, with GWP (AR4) 675 and ODP 0.

A single-component HFC with roughly one-third the GWP of R-410A, widely used in new residential and commercial air conditioners. Classified as mildly flammable (A2L); install according to safety standards.

Caution: R-32 is classified A2L (mildly flammable). Use it only in equipment designed for this class, never as a replacement in systems designed for A1 refrigerants, and have trained technicians handle it.

An ORAFON-brand product from Oranoss Co., Ltd., backed by TDS/SDS documents and technical advice on choosing the right refrigerant. Contact us for pack sizes, stock and a quotation.

FAQ
Which refrigerants can ORAFON R-32 replace?
R-410A, R-22, in equipment designed for A2L refrigerants. Before converting, check compressor oil and equipment compatibility with the manufacturer or ask the ORAFON team.
Is it flammable? How safe is it?
It is classified A2L under ASHRAE 34. Mildly flammable: use only in equipment designed for A2L.
