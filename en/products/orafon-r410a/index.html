ORAFON R-410A is an HFC Blend refrigerant (near-azeotropic blend), ASHRAE number R-410A, composition R-32 / R-125 (50/50). It is classified A1 under ASHRAE 34, with GWP (AR4) 2088 and ODP 0.

A near-azeotropic, non-flammable (A1) HFC blend widely used in air conditioners and heat pumps designed for R-410A. Operates at higher pressure than R-22.
