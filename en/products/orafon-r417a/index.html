ORAFON R-417A is an HFC Blend refrigerant (zeotropic blend), ASHRAE number R-417A, composition R-125 / R-134a / R-600 (46.6/50/3.4). It is classified A1 under ASHRAE 34, with GWP (AR4) 2346 and ODP 0.

A zeotropic, non-flammable (A1) HFC blend designed as a retrofit replacement for R-22 in existing AC and refrigeration systems; in many cases it works with the existing compressor oil (confirm with the equipment manufacturer).

An ORAFON-brand product from Oranoss Co., Ltd., backed by TDS/SDS documents and technical advice on choosing the right refrigerant. Contact us for pack sizes, stock and a quotation.
