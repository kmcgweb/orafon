Solstice® N40 (R-448A) is a non-flammable HFO blend developed as an alternative to R-404A, R-507, R-22 and the R-407 series, including interim blends, in commercial refrigeration. It suits contractors, distributors, service teams and building operators for both new installations and retrofits of low- and medium-temperature systems.

With an A1 non-flammable classification it supports familiar handling practices, while its performance profile supports reliable operation across cold storage and food retail.

Compared with R-404A, Solstice N40 offers 68% lower GWP and can reduce energy use in qualifying systems — a strong efficiency and operating-cost case for teams planning long-term refrigerant strategy.

FAQ
Which refrigerants can Solstice N40 replace?
R-404A, R-507, R-22 and the R-407 series, including interim blends, in commercial refrigeration — for both new systems and retrofits.
Is it flammable? How safe is it?
It is classified A1 (non-flammable) under ASHRAE, so technicians and service teams can use familiar handling practices.
Does it really lower GWP and save energy?
Compared with R-404A it has 68% lower GWP and can reduce energy consumption in qualifying systems, lowering long-term operating costs.
Which pack sizes are available?
Four sizes — Jug 11.3 kg, Cylinder 45 kg, Half Ton 386 kg and Ton 658 kg. Contact ORAFON to check stock and request a quotation.
