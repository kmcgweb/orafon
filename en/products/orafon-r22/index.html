ORAFON R-22 is an HCFC refrigerant (single component), ASHRAE number R-22, composition CHClF₂ (Chlorodifluoromethane). It is classified A1 under ASHRAE 34, with GWP (AR4) 1810 and ODP 0.055.

A single-component, non-flammable (A1) HCFC that was the mainstay of older air conditioners and cold rooms. Because it depletes ozone it is being phased out under the Montreal Protocol and is now mainly used to service existing systems.

Note: HCFCs are being phased out under the Montreal Protocol and imports are controlled. Use for servicing existing systems and plan a long-term move to alternatives.

An ORAFON-brand product from Oranoss Co., Ltd., backed by TDS/SDS documents and technical advice on choosing the right refrigerant. Contact us for pack sizes, stock and a quotation.
