Solstice® R-449A is an HFO Blend refrigerant (zeotropic blend), ASHRAE number R-449A, composition R-32 / R-125 / R-1234yf / R-134a (24.3/24.7/25.3/25.7). It is classified A1 under ASHRAE 34, with GWP (AR4) 1397 and ODP 0.

A non-flammable (A1) HFO/HFC blend replacing R-404A, R-507A and R-22 in low- and medium-temperature commercial refrigeration, with about 64% lower GWP than R-404A; for new systems and retrofits.

Distributed in Thailand by ORAFON (Oranoss Co., Ltd.), an authorized Solstice® distributor, with TDS/SDS documents and technical support. Contact us for pack sizes, stock and a quotation.
