We are ORAFON

ORAFON is the refrigerant brand of Oranoss Co., Ltd. and an authorized distributor of Solstice®. We focus on accurate technical information so technicians and contractors can choose the right refrigerant for performance, safety and the environment.

Oranoss Co., Ltd.

Our mission

Deliver quality refrigerants and trustworthy advice that help customers plan cost-effective, sustainable systems.

The ORAFON brand

Our own refrigerant brand, covering the most widely used grades for air-conditioning and refrigeration.

Solstice® distributor

We distribute HFO-technology refrigerants from Solstice® Advanced Materials.

Caring for the environment

We support the transition to low-GWP refrigerants to reduce climate impact.
